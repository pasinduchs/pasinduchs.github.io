About Me

I focus on architectural drawings and digital models that make a project easier to understand and review. My Civil Engineering studies at the University of Peradeniya provide a technical foundation and shape my interest in accurate, well-presented design.

My current architectural work includes 2D drawing in AutoCAD and plan-based modelling in Revit. I model walls, doors, windows, stairs, floors, and roofs, and prepare clear model views and drawing documentation according to the agreed project scope.

I also explore introductory Python concepts and use Excel to organise information and work with data. For each brief, I value checking the supplied references, communicating clearly, and agreeing on scope and deliverables before work begins.

My longer-term interests include structural modelling and the wider applications of digital tools across Civil Engineering. I aim to continue broadening my technical understanding while keeping accuracy and clear visual communication central to my work.

During school, I took part in leadership activities, drawing competitions, sports, and other extracurricular events. Participating in a range of activities gave me experience beyond the classroom and helped me develop teamwork, creativity, discipline, and confidence—qualities I continue to value in learning and collaboration.
