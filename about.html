About Me

My journey in engineering is centred on building a strong technical foundation, developing practical skills, and exploring how digital tools can support the design and understanding of the built environment. As a Civil Engineering undergraduate at the University of Peradeniya, I am gradually connecting what I learn through my studies with practical design and modelling work.

My current work with Revit focuses on developing architectural models from plans, including walls, doors, windows, stairs, floors, and roofs. Through this process, I am building my understanding of digital modelling and the importance of accuracy, detail, and clear visual representation.

Alongside Revit, I use AutoCAD for 2D drawing and have begun exploring Python programming concepts and Excel for organising information and working with data. These tools form part of my wider effort to develop useful technical skills alongside my engineering education.

I am particularly interested in structural modelling and the wider possibilities of Civil Engineering. As my knowledge develops, I hope to expand my technical abilities and explore new areas where engineering principles and digital tools can work together.

Beyond the classroom, my school years included participation in leadership activities, drawing competitions, sports, and a range of extracurricular events. Each offered a different way to learn through participation: working with others, exploring creative ideas, and committing time and attention to shared activities. Together, these experiences helped me build teamwork, creativity, discipline, and confidence. I continue to value these qualities in my personal and academic development, and bring them to the way I approach new challenges, learning opportunities, and collaboration with others.
